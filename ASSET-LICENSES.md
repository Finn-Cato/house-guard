# App image photograph

The app-store images `assets/images/small.png`, `large.png` and `xlarge.png`
are cropped exports of **“A modern house illuminated at twilight”** by
**Alice Chikova (@alice_chkv)**, published 29 June 2025 on Unsplash.

- Photograph: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-modern-house-illuminated-at-twilight-mKAJ8TvzpNE

No endorsement by the photographer or by Unsplash is implied. The original

The images contain no added text, app logo or Homey branding. To reproduce
the exports, download the source photo at width 2000 (JPEG), then run
`node scripts/branding.js <path-to-source.jpg>`. The script exports the crop
in Homey's 250 × 175, 500 × 350 and 1000 × 700 app-image sizes.

House Guard's app and driver SVG icons are separate source artwork and are
not part of this photograph. Contributor credits are recorded in the app
manifest and Git history.
