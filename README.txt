House Guard brings your home's daily routines and protection together. Let lights and alarms follow who is home, welcome the first person arriving and settle the house for the night. Guest mode keeps the home comfortable while visitors stay behind.

Keep track of intrusion, fire and water alerts in one place, with notifications for the people you choose and optional camera images. Guided setup helps you use your existing Homey devices, while Flow lets you tailor routines to your household.
